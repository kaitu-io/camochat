Security

Trust the maths, not a promise

Our privacy doesn't rest on "we won't look". For text there is nowhere for us to look.

Keys never leave your phone

Your identity is a key pair made on your device. It isn't tied to a phone number or email, and it is never uploaded.

A fresh key for every message

Each message key is derived one way from a chain and thrown away after use, so a ciphertext opens once, and keys can't be run backwards to older messages.

What CamoChat does not hide The chat app still knows who you talk to and when — only the content is sealed. Anyone holding your unlocked phone can read your history in CamoChat. The ratchet moves keys forward but does not yet heal itself after a phone is compromised.

FAQ

Questions

Things people usually ask first.

Does my friend need CamoChat too?

Yes. End-to-end encryption needs a program on both ends that can decrypt. But neither of you has to leave the chat app you already use.

Does CamoChat send my messages?

Not text. CamoChat has no message server and text never goes online; you choose how the ciphertext travels. Voice, photos and video are the exception: they are encrypted on your phone and stored as ciphertext on our relay, and the chat carries a short link. The app marks them expired after 24 hours and the relay deletes them within about 1–2 days.

Can you read my messages?

No, and not because we promise. Text never reaches any server, ours included. Media reaches our relay only as ciphertext, and the keys exist only on your phone and your friend's.

Do I need an account?

No. Your identity is a key pair on your device. No phone number, no email, no contact upload. You name your contacts yourself.

Why does the ciphertext look like Chinese characters?

Chat apps mangle long random strings, links and unusual symbols. CamoChat packs the ciphertext into a compact run of characters that survives copy-paste through common messengers, behind a 🔒 so you can spot it.

What happens if I lose my phone?

Keys never leave the device, so there is no cloud copy to restore from. Export your recovery phrase in the app ahead of time and you can restore your identity on a new phone; otherwise pair again with your contacts.
