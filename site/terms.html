一句话版本
  • 首次使用前，你需要阅读并同意本条款。
  • 零容忍：违法、色情、暴力、仇恨、骚扰、诈骗等内容，以及任何滥用行为，一律禁止。
  • 你可以在「联系人详情 → 拉黑」屏蔽某人，也可以「长按消息 → 举报」。

1. 适用对象与接受方式

本条款适用于所有使用陈仓（CamoChat）的人。首次使用前，App 会请你阅读并点击同意；不同意则不能使用。我们修改条款后，可能会要求你重新同意。

2. 零容忍：禁止的内容与行为

我们对令人反感的内容和滥用他人的用户零容忍。你不得用陈仓发送、上传或传播：

  • 违法内容，或教唆、协助违法的内容；
  • 色情、淫秽内容，尤其是涉及未成年人的任何内容；
  • 血腥、暴力内容，或威胁他人人身安全的内容；
  • 仇恨、歧视内容；
  • 骚扰、辱骂、跟踪、恐吓他人的内容；
  • 诈骗、欺诈、冒充他人的内容；
  • 其他滥用本服务的行为，例如批量骚扰陌生人、攻击或干扰我们的存储中转。

3. 拉黑与举报

  • 拉黑：打开「联系人详情 → 拉黑」。拉黑后，对方发来的消息不再显示；历史记录不会被删除，解除拉黑后会原样恢复。
  • 举报：长按消息 → 举报，App 会生成一封发给我们的邮件，由你查看并决定是否发出。邮件里有什么内容，完全由你自愿提供。

4. 我们如何处理举报

我们会在收到举报后 24 小时内处理。经核实违规的，我们会：

  • 删除被举报的内容，包括图片、语音、视频文件（这些文件以密文形式存放在我们的存储中转上）；
  • 终止违规用户使用本服务的权利。

也可以直接发邮件到 hi@allnationconnect.com 举报。

5. 端到端加密的说明

陈仓的消息是端到端加密的：文字由你自己经聊天软件送出，我们看不到文字；图片、语音、视频以密文形式在我们的存储中转上保留约 24 小时（云端约 1–2 天内自动删除），我们同样没有解密密钥。因此，我们处理举报时，依据的是举报人自愿发来的材料，以及据此定位到的被举报文件：举报图片、语音、视频消息时，App 会自动把该文件的编号（文件 ID）附在举报邮件里，我们凭这个编号在存储中转上找到并删除它。详见隐私政策。

6. 免责声明

陈仓按「现状」提供，我们不保证服务不中断或没有错误。用户发送的内容由发送者本人负责；在法律允许的范围内，我们不对用户之间的内容和行为承担责任，也不对因使用或无法使用本服务造成的间接损失负责。

7. 条款变更

我们可能更新本条款，并修改本页顶部的生效日期。重大变更会在 App 内或官网告知，并可能要求你重新同意。

The short version

  • Zero tolerance: illegal, sexual, violent, hateful, harassing or fraudulent content, and any abuse of the service, is prohibited.
  • You can block someone with Contact details → Block, or report a message with long-press a message → Report.
  • We act on reports within 24 hours: we remove the reported content and terminate the offending user's right to use the service.

1. Who these terms apply to, and how you accept them

These terms apply to everyone who uses CamoChat. Before you first use the app, it asks you to read and agree; if you do not agree, you cannot use it. If we change the terms, we may ask you to agree again.

2. Zero tolerance: prohibited content and conduct

We have zero tolerance for objectionable content and for abusive users. You must not send, upload or spread with CamoChat:

  • illegal content, or content that encourages or helps others to break the law;
  • pornographic or obscene content, and in particular anything involving minors;
  • graphic or violent content, or content that threatens anyone's safety;
  • hateful or discriminatory content;
  • content that harasses, abuses, stalks or intimidates others;
  • scams, fraud or impersonation;
  • any other abuse of the service, such as mass-harassing strangers or attacking or interfering with our storage relay.

3. Blocking and reporting

  • Block: open Contact details → Block. Messages from a blocked contact are no longer shown; history is not deleted and is restored exactly as it was when you unblock.
  • Report: long-press a message → Report. The app prepares an email to us for you to review and decide whether to send. What the email contains is entirely what you choose to provide.

4. How we handle reports

We act on reports within 24 hours of receiving them. When a violation is confirmed, we will:

  • remove the reported content, including photo, voice or video files (stored as ciphertext on our storage relay);
  • terminate the offending user's right to use the service.

You can also report directly by emailing hi@allnationconnect.com.

5. A note on end-to-end encryption

CamoChat messages are end-to-end encrypted: you carry text through your chat app yourself and we cannot see it; photos, voice and videos are kept as ciphertext on our storage relay for about 24 hours (deleted automatically from the cloud within about 1–2 days), and we have no decryption keys for them either. So when we handle a report, we rely on the material the reporter chooses to send us and on the reported file we locate from it: when you report a photo, voice or video message, the app automatically attaches that file's ID to the report email, and we use this ID to find and delete the file on the storage relay. See the Privacy Policy for details.

CamoChat is provided "as is", and we do not guarantee that the service will be uninterrupted or error-free. Users are responsible for the content they send. To the extent permitted by law, we are not responsible for content or conduct between users, nor for indirect losses arising from using or being unable to use the service.

We may update these terms and will change the effective date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be announced in the app or on our website, and we may ask you to agree again.
